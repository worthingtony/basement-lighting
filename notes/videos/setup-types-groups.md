# SoundSwitch videos: setup, fixture types and groups

Source: official SoundSwitch YouTube auto-captions in `/Users/tonyw/Dropbox/SoundSwitch/youtube-captions/txt/` (95 files). Read in full: the 15 videos in the brief, plus every transcript with hits for group / primary / secondary / tertiary / category / wash / mover / fixture type / cell / master track / venue (about 30 more). Captions are machine-made, so wording is sometimes garbled.

Labels: **STATED** means the video says or shows it plainly. **INFERRED** is my reading. Year = upload date. Videos from 2016-2019 describe SoundSwitch 1.x-2.1. Tony runs 2.11.

## Short answer to the key question

| Setting | What the videos say it does | Does it decide which cues a fixture gets? |
|---|---|---|
| **Type / category** (Wash, Mover, Spot, Multi Cell, Strobe-Blinder, each Primary / Secondary / Tertiary) | **Autoscript ("automation") reads it.** Each category gets different effects. The demo sets two identical washes to Wash Primary and Wash Secondary, and they come out of Autoscript with different effects. Change a category, re-run Autoscript, and the result changes. | **Only when Autoscript runs**, on a track or an Autoloop (Autoloops since the 2023 video). No video says the category changes how the Main Track, a hand-built Autoloop or a Static Look plays. |
| **Group 1-4** (buttons in the DMX patch) | 2.3 calls these **"control groups"**. They feed the **group intensity faders** at the bottom of the Performance screen, and the Control One touch strip's group buttons. A group dims together, and a fixture alone in a group can be dimmed on its own. | **Not stated anywhere.** No video ties Group 1-4 to Autoscript, Autoloops or cue placement. They look like a live dimmer layer only. |
| **Fixture group** (Insert > Fixture Group, a folder in the workspace) | A different thing. It gives a **Group Track** that overrides the Main Track for its members. A chase dropped "on the group" is spread across the fixtures inside it. All-in-one fixtures get one automatically. | Yes, for editing: it sets what a chase spreads across and what a group-track override covers. |

- **Do fixtures that share a type and group "move as one"?** No video says so. The videos only say that fixtures with **different** categories get **different** Autoscript effects, and that fixtures move together because they all inherit the **Main Track** (one Position Cue, with each fixture using its own stored pan/tilt for that named position). INFERRED: within one category, Autoscript gives the fixtures the same kind of effect, but a chase or Position Chase still staggers them. Nothing shows identical cues coming from category + group.
- **Primary vs Secondary vs Tertiary:** the videos show only that Primary and Secondary get different effects ("primary effects" on one light, "secondary effects" on the other). They never describe what each tier gets. Each Autoscript preset has an **info (i) button** that lists what it does per fixture category and per song section. That's the place to read it in the app.
- **Don't mix this up with the Autoscript *color* Primary / Secondary / Tertiary / Highlight.** Those are palette swatches in the Advanced Autoscript dialog. No video links them to fixture categories.
- **Multi-cell:** an all-in-one fixture (GigBar) arrives as a fixture group, with one fixture track per part (derbies, washes, movers) and **one category for the whole unit**. The videos never mention a **MASTER cell**.
- **Empty track:** **it follows the next level up.** The Main Track drives every light. A Group Track overrides it for the fixtures inside the group, and a fixture track overrides both. A fixture with nothing on its own track keeps following the Main Track (or its Group Track). To stop it following, you create an override on its track.

## 1. Fixture categories (type dropdown) and Autoscript

- **STATED** - In the DMX map, each fixture's "fixture category" is what the automation uses to pick its effects, so different categories get different effects. Example: top washes as Wash Primary, bottom washes as Wash Secondary. *AutoScripting Explained (2021)*, [05:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0b22G5i4Ut4&t=328)
- **STATED** - In the same demo the GigBar Move is left as Mover Primary and the top lights as Wash Primary. He suggests changing categories and re-running to see what happens. *AutoScripting Explained (2021)*, [05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0b22G5i4Ut4&t=359)
- **STATED** - After Autoscript, the left top and left bottom lights have different effects on their fixture tracks "because they have been categorized differently": primary effects on one, secondary effects on the other. *AutoScripting Explained (2021)*, [07:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0b22G5i4Ut4&t=468)
- **STATED** - In 2.3, Autoscript started applying more effects based on the fixture category. Changing a light's category and rescripting changes the effects on that light. *What's New in SoundSwitch 2.3 (2021)*, [03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HSue8aJVn8c&t=232) to [04:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HSue8aJVn8c&t=266)
- **STATED** - Each preset's information icon shows the settings it applies to the lights, the fixture categories, and the song sections. *AutoScripting Explained (2021)*, [12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0b22G5i4Ut4&t=734)
- **STATED** - The category options listed in 2020 were Wash Light, spotlight, Multi-cell Fixture, or Strobe/Blinder. For a GigBar 2 he recommends Wash Primary for most cases. *GigBar 2 Tutorial (2020)*, [00:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tUO9Tp234zk&t=39)
  - **Version note:** this list leaves out Mover and Smoke. The GigBar 2 has no movers, and Mover Primary does appear in 2021 ([05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0b22G5i4Ut4&t=359)). It's an incomplete list, not a contradiction.
- **STATED** - Since 2.2, Autoscript has applied effects to specific fixtures (chases, intensity overrides, Attribute Cues), not just to the Master Track. *SoundSwitch 2.2 (2020)*, [01:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wis8O1gTWP8&t=72). Even in 2018 the automation was putting effects on fixture tracks as well as the Master Track. *Scripting Session: Custom Scripted Tracks Pt. 1 (2018)*, [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MWmxn4Ym2Ds&t=3)
- **STATED** - Before 2023, Autoloops had effects only in the Main Track. Running Autoscript on an Autoloop now spreads effects across the fixture tracks and "takes into consideration" the lights in the venue. The stock Bank 4 loop shown had nothing on its fixture tracks. *Autoloop AutoScripting (2023)*, [00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5alSfsuWigQ&t=32) to [01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5alSfsuWigQ&t=96)
  - **INFERRED:** so the category matters for Autoloops only when you Autoscript them. Tony's hand-built loops (Bank 1) aren't affected by it.
- **STATED** - Autoscript's Advanced dialog has a color scheme: Primary, Secondary and Tertiary colors plus a Highlight color (white in the demo). *GigBar 2 Tutorial (2020)*, [07:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tUO9Tp234zk&t=432)
  - **INFERRED:** these are palette slots. No video says the "Primary color" goes to "Primary" fixtures. Treat any link between them as unknown.
- **STATED** - The Advanced dialog also sets base, pulse and bridge intensity, movement speed (how often Position Cues are placed), color change speed and effect (chase) speed. It also picks which Position and Attribute Cues are placed, in what order, for intro / main one / main two / bridge / middle / outro, with randomize and change-and-hold options. *AutoScripting Explained (2021)*, [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0b22G5i4Ut4&t=764) to [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0b22G5i4Ut4&t=862)
- **STATED** - Adding a cue folder to the automation's "used" list makes Autoscript use every cue inside it. *SoundSwitch 2.1 (2019)*, [01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8JF6C-kpLxo&t=101) to [02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8JF6C-kpLxo&t=135)
- **STATED** - Phrase sections can be set to "disable scripting", so a re-run of Autoscript leaves them alone. *Autoscript Phrase Editing (2023)*, [02:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m_iJS6RE01Q&t=154)

- **STATED** - The group buttons on the touch strip give groups 1 and 3, and a double tap gives groups 2 and 4. ## 4. Track hierarchy: what an empty track does

- **STATED** - The Main Track at the bottom controls every light. A Group Track overrides it for its members, and a fixture track overrides it for that one fixture. That's how you build a color chase or give one light its own color. *Advanced Features (2021)*, [02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_YZMGEKhjw&t=176) to [04:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_YZMGEKhjw&t=242)
- **STATED** - Intensity, strobe and movement can each be overridden per fixture: create an intensity override and draw a curve, or create a Position override and drop in another Position Cue. *Advanced Features (2021)*, [04:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_YZMGEKhjw&t=273)
- **STATED** - Two outer movers with their own Position override (a circle) do that, while the GigBar movers, which have nothing on their tracks, follow the Main Track's figure-eight. *Movement & Attribute Cues (2021)*, [09:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uSRTHbTTSaQ&t=545) to [09:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uSRTHbTTSaQ&t=575)
- **STATED** - A Position override is what tells a light to stop following the Master Track. You make one by selecting on a mover's position track and choosing Create Position Override. *Scripting Session: Advanced Chase Effects (2017)*, [01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxU4nqV5ebw&t=86)
- **STATED** - Setting strobe to 0 on a Group Track stops the group's lights strobing, but a fixture track outside it still strobes with the Master Track. *Adding Strobe Effects (2019)*, [00:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMtnwXKctAc&t=40) to [01:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMtnwXKctAc&t=73); the same in *Editing Colors, Strobe & Overrides (2019)*, [01:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vjgRslXhxvk&t=111)
- **STATED** - Cmd+Delete clears the data inside an override and leaves the override section in place. Shift+Cmd+Delete clears the section too. On the Master Track, Shift+Cmd+Delete also removes the Attribute Cues. *Quick Tips: Deleting Lighting Info (2019)*, [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tNgaXXaRxqc&t=3) to [01:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tNgaXXaRxqc&t=81); also *Advanced Features (2021)*, [05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_YZMGEKhjw&t=305)
- **INFERRED** - The videos never say what an override section does once you've emptied it. Repo note `design-research.md` (2026-10-06) records that a flat 0 intensity override gives darkness. That fits, but no video confirms it.
- **STATED** - The Main Track is identical in every venue, so a scripted track or Autoloop plays a basic, coherent show on any rig without re-scripting. *Advanced Features (2021)*, [01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_YZMGEKhjw&t=67) to [01:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_YZMGEKhjw&t=103); *Scripting Session 1 (2016)*, [08:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EETBfocNxOY&t=492)
- **STATED / INFERRED** - In your own venue, the stock Autoloops' fixture tracks need customizing (chases, color changes). Only the shipped GigBar Move venue has fixture-track effects. *Autoloops Explained (2021)*, [03:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dwe6g-qczjI&t=189). INFERRED: fixture and group tracks belong to a venue, and only the Main Track is shared.
- **STATED** - In a Static Look, a fixture you activate follows the look. A fixture you leave deactivated keeps following the Autoloop underneath. *Static Looks Explained (2021)*, [02:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K-wQj_oVWt8&t=127) and [05:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K-wQj_oVWt8&t=315) to [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K-wQj_oVWt8&t=346); *Getting Started (2021)*, [08:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8zbq-WVNN9s&t=484)
- **STATED** - In the Static Look editor you can select every fixture "of the same type" by clicking the top level, or pick fixtures one by one. *Static Looks Explained (2021)*, [01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K-wQj_oVWt8&t=97). INFERRED: "top level" probably means the group or fixture heading in that list. It isn't clearly the category.

## 6. Patching and the DMX map

- **STATED** - The DMX map arrived in 2.0. It has an "All Manufacturers" search (type the full fixture name). You add a fixture by dragging a mapping onto the universe, double-clicking it, or using Add Fixture / Add Multiple. *DMX Map (2019)*, [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5KKlSAAynbM&t=3) to [00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5KKlSAAynbM&t=33)
- **STATED** - Moving a mapping on the grid updates the fixture track's DMX address. Cross-patched or overlapping fixtures are drawn as an overlap, and none should overlap. *DMX Map (2019)*, [01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5KKlSAAynbM&t=65). Since 2.3 the DMX icon turns yellow when anything is cross-patched. *What's New in SoundSwitch 2.3 (2021)*, [04:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HSue8aJVn8c&t=296); *AutoScripting Explained (2021)*, [03:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0b22G5i4Ut4&t=228)
- **STATED** - You delete fixtures by right-clicking them on the grid, or by right-clicking the fixture menu on the right. Fixture menus grey out when the fixture isn't on the universe tab you're viewing. *DMX Map (2019)*, [01:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5KKlSAAynbM&t=99)
- **STATED** - Patching means matching the light's own DMX mode and address. For example, a light in 6-channel mode at address 500 gets the 6-channel mapping dropped at 500. A test color override (blue, then red) shows whether it's patched right. A wrong address shows a wrong color, or nothing. *Back to Basics (2021)*, [09:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J0C19a447kI&t=590) to [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J0C19a447kI&t=687)
- **STATED** - SoundSwitch handles two universes (1024 channels). *Back to Basics (2021)*, [03:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J0C19a447kI&t=226). To put a fixture on universe 2, use the DMX map or type "2:" in front of its address. *Enttec DMX USB Pro MK2 Connection Guide (2019)*, [00:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r16bvcoa04M&t=37)
- **STATED** - Preferences > Hardware lists the Control One, where you can assign universes 1 and 2 or set it inactive. *Getting Started with the Control One (2021)*, [08:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eHgkltVdG-I&t=494)
- **STATED** - Some fixtures (the GigBar Move) have a "simple" mapping made to work well with SoundSwitch. *Getting Started (2021)*, [02:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8zbq-WVNN9s&t=134)

## 7. Fixture library and swapping fixture mappings

## 8. Positions and inverted movers

- **STATED** - Setting the pan/tilt invert in one moving head's own setup menu lets you select both heads in the Position editor and drag them as a mirrored pair. *Quick Tips: Inverted Moving Heads (2017)*, [00:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PQLgQaZdB0&t=3). - **STATED** - Lights mounted sideways or facing down use different corners of the X/Y pad. Always drag a mover outward, away from the wall behind it, so it never swings backward. *Movement & Attribute Cues (2021)*, [04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uSRTHbTTSaQ&t=284) to [05:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uSRTHbTTSaQ&t=320)

- **STATED** - Editing a Position (pan/tilt) and pressing Apply updates every Position Cue that uses it, in all Autoloops and scripted tracks, with no re-scripting. *Movement & Attribute Cues (2021)*, [05:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uSRTHbTTSaQ&t=355); *Getting Started (2021)*, [14:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8zbq-WVNN9s&t=849)

  - **INFERRED:** once the hazer is patched, any pre-placed Attribute Cue that holds a smoke value would fire it. That supports checking and zeroing the global smoke cue, as the CLAUDE.md open item says.
- **STATED** - Editing an Attribute Cue updates every flag that uses it. Duplicating a cue copies its settings (since 2.3). *Movement & Attribute Cues (2021)*, [07:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uSRTHbTTSaQ&t=448); *What's New in SoundSwitch 2.3 (2021)*, [04:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HSue8aJVn8c&t=296)

## 10. Venues, projects and the Control One copy

- **STATED** - Venues organize lighting rigs (for example big / medium / small, or one per club). The Main Track is the same in all of them. *Advanced Features (2021)*, [01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_YZMGEKhjw&t=67)
- **STATED** - Projects arrived in 2.3: File > New / Open / Save / Save As / Export Project. A project holds its venues, Autoloops and scripted tracks. Export can include some or all venues, with or without the lighting tracks. *SoundSwitch Projects (2021)*, [00:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hR7CoZHfx14&t=39) to [02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hR7CoZHfx14&t=148)
- **STATED** - File > Export to Control One stores one venue (with its Autoloop data) on the device and **overwrites whatever is already there**. In Performance mode that venue then shows a USB icon, and picking it imports the project from the Control One. *Getting Started with the Control One (2021)*, [06:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eHgkltVdG-I&t=362) to [07:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eHgkltVdG-I&t=429)
  - This matches Tony's rule of always using the disk venue, since the USB venue is the Control One's copy.
